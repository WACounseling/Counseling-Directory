A starting point for finding outside therapists, psychologists, and specialists near you — searchable by concern, location, setting, and clinician preference.

Supporting a student's mental health is a group project

Students are safer and better supported when families, school counselors, and outside clinicians all work together. This directory is one part of that team — not a replacement for it.

We encourage every family to start by talking with your student's grade-level school counselor. They can help coordinate support at school, share what they're already seeing, and offer specific recommendations that go beyond this list.

Use this resource, alongside your counselor's guidance, to find additional support outside of school that fits your family's needs.

About This List

Woodward Academy and its counseling staff are not affiliated with, and do not formally endorse, employ, supervise, or take responsibility for, the clinicians or practices listed below. This directory is provided solely as a convenience to help students and families identify potential resources — it is not a specific recommendation or referral for any individual student.

Listings have been compiled from providers that current or former Woodward families have worked with, practices that have been suggested to our department, or clinicians our counselors have consulted or collaborated with in supporting students. Inclusion on this list does not constitute a guarantee of quality of care, and past positive experiences with a provider do not guarantee similar results for another student or family. Not every provider is the right fit for every family, and families are encouraged to use their own judgment — including consulting with a pediatrician, licensed clinician, or insurance carrier — when selecting care.

This list is not comprehensive and may not reflect current availability, credentials, or practice status. For a broader and more up-to-date range of options, families may also wish to consult their pediatrician, their insurance carrier's provider network, or directories such as Psychology Today (psychologytoday.com).
